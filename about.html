About GEN-CARE Ghana

Gender Equality, Climate Action & Environmental Rights Ghana (GEN-CARE Ghana) is a non- profit, non-partisan organization committed to advancing gender equality, promoting climate action, and protecting environmental rights in Ghana.

The organization works to empower women, girls, and communities through advocacy, capacity building, policy engagement, and community-based initiatives that address gender inequality, climate change, and environmental sustainability.

GEN-CARE Ghana seeks to strengthen inclusive leadership, support grassroots participation in decision-making, and promote sustainable development by ensuring that gender perspectives and environmental justice are integrated into policies, programs, and community actions.

Who We Are

We are a community-focused NGO working with local partners to deliver sustainable development solutions across Ghana.

Our Vision

A just and sustainable society where gender equality, climate justice, and environmental rights are fully realized.

Our Mission

To empower youth, women, and vulnerable communities through education, advocacy, leadership development, and partnerships to advance gender equality and climate action.

Our Core Values

Equality and Inclusion

We ensure that every individual is treated fairly and has equal access to opportunities, regardless of background or circumstance.

Youth Leadership

We empower young people to take active leadership roles in driving change and shaping the future of their communities.

Integrity and Accountability

We act with honesty and strong ethical principles, taking full responsibility for our actions, decisions, and the resources entrusted to us.

Transparency

We build trust through openness and clear communication.

Environmental Sustainability

We promote practices that protect the environment and ensure the responsible use of resources for future generations.

Collaboration and Partnerships

We believe in working together with communities, organizations, and stakeholders to achieve greater and more sustainable impact.
